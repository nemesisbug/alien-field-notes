An alien tourist's one-line review of planet Earth, written by `main.py`
to `/output/entry.txt` for the make.fun bounty
[Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review](https://make.fun/bounty/u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000).

## The entry

> Earth: two days in, a goose has claimed my ship, three locals asked me
> for directions, and the snacks are unreal. 4/5 — would invade again.

Two days on Earth, as the tourist saw it: the ship lost to a goose, the
locals mistaking the visitor for a local, and snacks good enough to come
back for. One joke, one verdict, one rating — the punch lands last.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` if set).
The line is 139 characters, inside the bounty gate of 1–200 characters,
and `main.py` asserts that gate itself before writing.
